Company Win Loss Legend
  • General Info
  • All amounts shown are in MYR (Malaysian ringgit).
  • This report contains only Won/Lose/Draw tickets.
  • The report is grouped by Product.
  • The Virtual Rate is applied for Sportsbook. Other products are based on Actual Rate.
  • For more historical data, please click to HISTORY REPORT to view report with data range up to 3 months.
  • For the company winloss, a red positive number indicates a loss for company, whereas a black negative number indicates a win.
  • For agency winloss, a black positive number indicates a win for agency, whereas a black negative number indicates a loss.
  • Term definition and explanation
  • Product: list all the Product having bet tickets within selected date range.
  • Member Winloss: member winloss excluding commission.
  • SMA Win Loss: agency winloss excluding commission.
  • Commission: super commission received from company.
  • Company Income: the company net winloss
    • Company Income (Real): the company net winloss based on actual rate after deducting super commission.
    • Company Income (Virtual): the company net winloss based on virtual rate after deducting super commission.
  • Variance: the difference between Line Total Real and Line Total Virtual.
  • Company Turnover: the Company’s Turnover after Position Taking (exclude MBC - RNG Casino, Bingo products).
    • Company Turnover (Real): the Company’s Turnover based on actual rate.
    • Company Turnover (Virtual): the Company’s Turnover based on virtual rate.
  • Margin: % percentage of Line Total over Turnover.
    • Margin (Real): percentage of Line Total Real over Turnover Real.
    • Margin (Virtual): percentage of Line Total Virtual over Turnover Virtual.
  • Sportsbook Total: winloss of Sportsbook in total (Sportsbook Live, Sportsbook Non-Live, Parlay and Outright)
  • Grand Total= Sportsbook Total + Number Game + Live Casino + Casino + Virtual Sports + Keno + Racing + Bingo + Bettrade + Colossus Bets.​
